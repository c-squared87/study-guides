# Day 1 — Introduction to IT

## What is Information Technology?

Information technology is the use of computers, networks, software, and related technologies to create, store, process, and communicate information.

## Key Concepts

- Hardware
- Software
- Networks
- Data
- Information systems

## Important Terms

**Hardware**  
The physical components of a computer system.

**Software**  
Programs and instructions that tell hardware what to do.

**Network**  
A collection of connected devices that can communicate with each other.
